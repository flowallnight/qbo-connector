Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This application is a private QuickBooks Online connector used by its owner to access the owner's own QuickBooks Online company data through Intuit's authorized API and OAuth services.

The application is not offered to external users and does not sell personal or accounting data. Any QuickBooks data accessed through the connector is used only for the owner's accounting and data-management purposes.

Authentication is handled through Intuit's OAuth process. QuickBooks credentials should not be stored in this public website or repository. Any API tokens or accounting data used by local Python scripts should be stored privately and not committed to a public repository.

Use of QuickBooks Online and Intuit services is also subject to Intuit's applicable privacy policy and terms.
